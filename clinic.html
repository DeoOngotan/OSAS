About the Clinic

A Calm Space for Your Well-Being

The ISPSC Tagudin Campus Health Clinic operates under the Office of Student Affairs and Services to provide primary health care services to all students, faculty, and staff within the campus. Our clinic is staffed by qualified health professionals committed to delivering prompt, compassionate, and student-centered care.

Whether you're feeling unwell, need a first-aid treatment, require a medical certificate, or simply want health advice — the clinic is a safe, calm space where you are always welcome. All services are provided free of charge to enrolled students.

What We Offer

Health Services

From first aid to health education — we've got your well-being covered on campus.

First Aid & Emergency Care

Immediate first-aid treatment for injuries, fainting, allergic reactions, and other urgent health concerns that occur within the campus premises.

General Medical Consultation

Basic consultations for common illnesses such as colds, fever, headaches, stomach aches, and other health complaints that affect your daily campus life.

Dental Services

Basic dental care including tooth extraction, oral examination, and dental health consultations. Students experiencing toothaches or oral discomfort are encouraged to visit the clinic.

Medical Certificate Issuance

Issue medical certificates for absenteeism, return-to-school clearance, scholarship requirements, and other purposes that require documentation of a student's health status.

Medicines & Basic Supplies

Essential medicines (analgesics, antihistamines, antacids, wound care) and medical supplies are available at the clinic for student use at no cost during consultations.

Pre-Participation Health Screening

Health assessment and physical examination for students participating in intramural sports, off-campus activities, and other physically demanding school programs.

Health Education & Counseling

Provide information and guidance on personal health, hygiene, reproductive health, nutrition, and disease prevention through one-on-one consultation and campus health campaigns.

Referral Services

When cases require advanced treatment beyond the clinic's capacity, our health staff will refer students to the nearest government hospital or appropriate specialist with proper documentation.

Stay Well

Simple Health Tips for Students

Good health starts with small daily habits. Here are a few things to keep in mind during the school year.

Get Enough Rest

Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep. Rest is essential for concentration, memory retention, and your immune system.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water a day, especially during hot weather and physical activities on campus.

Eat Balanced Meals

Don't skip meals. A balanced diet keeps your energy levels steady and helps you stay focused throughout the day.

Move Your Body

Take short walks between classes or join an intramural sport. Regular movement reduces stress and boosts mood.

Talk When You're Struggling

Mental health is health. If you're feeling overwhelmed, talk to a friend, counselor, or visit the Guidance Office — you don't have to carry it alone.

Practice Good Hygiene

Wash hands regularly, especially before meals. Keep shared surfaces clean and practice respiratory etiquette to prevent the spread of illness on campus.

When to Come

When Should You Visit the Clinic?

Don't wait until things get serious. Come in anytime you feel any of the following.

Fever or Chills

If your body temperature is elevated or you feel unusually cold, come in immediately for assessment.

Headache or Dizziness

Persistent headaches or sudden dizziness during class or activities warrant a clinic visit.

Wounds or Injuries

Any cut, abrasion, sprain, or injury from physical activity should be assessed and treated promptly.

Stomach Pain or Nausea

Gastrointestinal symptoms like vomiting, diarrhea, or stomach cramps need medical attention.

Tooth or Gum Pain

Dental pain can be debilitating. Our dental officer can provide relief and basic dental care.

Needing a Medical Certificate

For absences, scholarship applications, or any official documentation of your health status.
