Who We Are

The Heart of Campus Life

The Student Organizations Unit under OSAS oversees all recognized student organizations at ISPSC Tagudin Campus. We handle the accreditation and recognition of student groups, coordinate their activities, provide mentorship, and ensure that all organizations operate within university policies and ethical standards.

From academic clubs and cultural groups to sports associations and civic organizations — we believe that student organizations are essential to holistic education. They develop leadership, teamwork, and a sense of community that goes beyond the classroom.

What We Do

Services for Student Organizations

Everything your organization needs to thrive on campus — we coordinate it all.

Organization Recognition & Accreditation

Process and approve the formal recognition of student organizations. We review constitutions, rosters, and activity plans to ensure compliance with ISPSC policies and CHED guidelines.

Activity Coordination & Permits

Assist student organizations in planning, coordinating, and securing permits for on-campus and off-campus activities, events, seminars, and community outreach programs.

Leadership Mentoring & Training

Provide leadership training seminars, mentoring sessions, and development workshops designed to equip student leaders with the skills needed for effective organization management.

Annual Renewal Processing

Guide organizations through the annual accreditation renewal process — reviewing financial reports, updated membership lists, accomplishment reports, and plans for the coming year.

Inter-Organization Coordination

Facilitate communication and collaboration between different student organizations, especially for joint events, campus-wide initiatives, and student government activities.

Policy Compliance & Advisory

Advise student organizations on proper governance, ethical conduct, financial management, and compliance with school policies, CHED memoranda, and relevant government regulations.

Campus-Wide Events Support

Coordinate and support major campus events like foundation days, leadership summits, intramurals, cultural presentations, and other student-driven celebrations that foster school spirit.

Community Service Programs

Encourage and support student organizations in conducting extension activities and community service projects that contribute to social development and the institution's community engagement goals.

Why Get Involved

Benefits of Joining an Organization

Student organizations are where leaders are made. Here's what you gain by getting involved.

Leadership Skills

Take on roles that build decision-making, communication, and team management abilities you'll carry for life.

Lasting Friendships

Connect with like-minded students who share your passions — building friendships and professional networks that last beyond graduation.

Portfolio & Resume Builder

Org membership, events organized, and positions held are valuable credentials that boost your résumé and graduate school applications.

Campus Representation

Represent your course, college, or advocacy in official campus forums, inter-school competitions, and community programs.

Personal Growth

Gain confidence, cultural awareness, and a broader perspective through exposure to diverse events, workshops, and collaborative projects.

Community Impact

Channel your org's energy into outreach programs, charity drives, and campus improvements that leave a real mark on the community.

Get Involved

How to Join or Register an Org

Whether you're joining an existing organization or founding a new one, here's how to get started.

1

Explore & Choose

Browse recognized organizations on the OSAS bulletin board or ask at the office. Find one that matches your interest or advocacy.

2

Contact the Org

Reach out to the organization's officers directly during recruitment drives or at the start of the semester.

3

Complete Membership

Fill out membership forms, pay dues if applicable, and attend orientation sessions required by the organization.

4

Register with OSAS

New organizations must submit a constitution, roster, and activity plan to OSAS for formal recognition before operating on campus.

Contact

Talk to the Student Organizations Unit

Visit or reach out to the OSAS office for accreditation, activity permits, or any organization-related concerns.
