Who We Are

Your Financial Guide on Campus

The Cashier's Office is the designated financial service unit of ISPSC Tagudin Campus under the Office of Student Affairs and Services. We handle the collection of tuition fees, miscellaneous charges, and other student payments in a secure, organized, and student-friendly manner.

Our staff are committed to providing accurate information about your fees, assisting with payment concerns, and ensuring that every transaction is properly recorded. Whether you have a question about your balance, need a payment receipt, or need guidance on scholarship deductions — we are here to help.

What We Handle

Cashier Services

Everything related to your school payments — handled here, handled right.

Tuition Fee Collection

Accept and process tuition payments for regular, irregular, and part-time students each semester, ensuring your enrollment is properly settled.

Official Receipt Issuance

Every payment is documented with an official receipt for your records. Replacement receipts and payment verification can also be requested from our office.

Scholarship Fee Deductions

Process and apply approved scholarship and grant deductions (e.g., CHED, UNIFAST, private sponsors) to your tuition balance accurately and on time.

Miscellaneous Fee Assessment

Breakdown and clarification of all miscellaneous fees including library, laboratory, student activity, and ID fees — so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Installment Plan Assistance

We accommodate students who need a payment arrangement. Our staff can guide you through the installment process and help you manage your balance semester by semester.

Refund Processing

Handle requests for tuition refunds due to withdrawal, over-payment, or enrollment cancellation in accordance with CHED policies and institutional guidelines.

Balance Inquiry

Check your outstanding balance, view your payment history, and get a clear breakdown of your remaining dues — no surprises at enrollment time.

Clearance Certification

Certify that your financial obligations with the institution are fully settled — a requirement for graduation, transferring, or requesting official school documents.

Good to Know

Payment Reminders

Keep these in mind before heading to the Cashier's window.

01

Bring Your School ID

Always present a valid school ID or enrollment form when transacting at the Cashier's window.

02

Pay on Scheduled Dates

Pay within the enrollment or payment deadlines to avoid late fees and complications with your enrollment status.

03

Keep Your Official Receipt

Always secure your official receipt after every payment. It serves as proof of your transaction and is required for clearance.

04

Verify Scholarship Credits

If you are a scholarship grantee, confirm with OSAS that your scholarship has been processed before making payment to avoid double payments.
