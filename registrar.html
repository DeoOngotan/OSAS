Senior High School Graduate

Complete list of required admission documents

  • 1
    ICAT Result
  • 2
    SF 9 (Senior High School Card)
  • 3
    Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 4
    Birth Certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA, formerly NSO). Marriage Certificate for married students.
  • 5
    3 pcs. Passport size, white background, with name tag (e.g., DELA CRUZ, Juan)
  • 6
    1 pc Long Folder & 1 pc Brown Envelope
📢

Should you have any questions or concerns regarding admissions or document processing, contact the Registrar.
