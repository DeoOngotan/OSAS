Who We Are

A Trusted Corner on Campus

The Guidance & Counseling Office is a student support service that provides a safe, non-judgmental environment where students can freely express their thoughts and feelings. Our licensed guidance counselors are dedicated to helping you navigate the challenges of student life — academic pressures, personal concerns, and career direction.

We believe every student deserves someone to talk to. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, confused about your future, or simply need someone to listen — our door is always open. All sessions are strictly confidential.

What We Offer

Programs & Activities

Everything we do is centered on your well-being, growth, and success as a student.

Individual Counseling

One-on-one private sessions with a licensed counselor to talk through academic stress, personal struggles, family concerns, or anything weighing on your mind.

Group Counseling

Facilitated group sessions that create a shared space for students facing similar challenges — building peer support, empathy, and collective resilience.

Career Guidance

Explore your strengths and interests through assessments and counseling that help you map a fulfilling career path aligned with your personality and goals.

Orientation Programs

Structured orientation sessions for new students to ease the transition into college life, covering academic expectations, campus resources, and student rights.

Mental Health Awareness

Seminars, workshops, and campaigns that reduce stigma, promote emotional well-being, and equip students with healthy coping strategies for everyday life.

Crisis Intervention

Immediate support for students in distress. Our counselors are trained to respond promptly and compassionately during difficult moments that require urgent attention.

Peer Facilitation

Training student volunteers as peer facilitators who support their fellow students, extending the reach of guidance services throughout the campus community.

Psychological Testing

Standardized assessments for self-awareness, aptitude, and interest profiling that help students better understand themselves and inform academic or career decisions.
