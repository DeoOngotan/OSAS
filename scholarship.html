Who We Are

Bridging Students to Opportunities

The Scholarship and Financial Assistance Unit of OSAS ISPSC Tagudin serves as the primary liaison between students and scholarship-granting bodies — both government agencies and private institutions. We process, coordinate, and monitor scholarship applications, renewals, and disbursements to ensure that deserving students receive the financial support they need.

Our office is committed to transparency, fairness, and timely processing of all scholarship-related concerns. Whether you're applying for the first time, renewing your grant, or simply exploring your options — we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Available Programs

Scholarship & Grant Programs

A range of programs available to ISPSC Tagudin students based on merit, financial need, and academic performance.

Government

Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES)

A CHED-administered subsidy under UNIFAST for students enrolled in State Universities and Colleges. Covers tuition and other school fees for qualified beneficiaries.

Administered by: CHED / UNIFAST
Government

Student Financial Assistance Programs (StuFAPs)

Covers a range of CHED-managed grants, scholarships, and loan programs designed to address financial barriers to higher education for deserving Filipino students.

Administered by: CHED
Government

PESFA (Private Education Student Financial Assistance)

While primarily for private HEIs, OSAS assists in endorsing eligible students and providing guidance on CHED scholarship programs applicable to SUC students.

Administered by: CHED
Performance-Based

Academic Excellence Grant

Awarded to students who demonstrate outstanding academic performance. Recipients must maintain a high GPA and comply with renewal requirements each semester.

Administered by: ISPSC Tagudin
Private

Private Sector Scholarships

Local government units, private companies, and civic organizations occasionally offer scholarships to ISPSC students. OSAS posts announcements and assists with endorsements and requirements.

Various Sponsors
Merit-Based

Indigenous Peoples (IP) Scholarship

A government-supported program for students belonging to indigenous cultural communities, providing educational assistance to preserve access to higher education among IP groups.

Administered by: NCIP / CHED
Application Process

How to Apply

Follow these steps to start your scholarship application through the OSAS office.

1

Check Eligibility & Announcements

Visit the OSAS office or monitor official ISPSC announcements for currently open scholarship programs. Requirements and deadlines vary per program.

2

Secure and Complete Requirements

Gather all required documents such as your grades, enrollment certificate, income certificate, barangay clearance, and other program-specific requirements.

3

Submit to OSAS Scholarship Unit

Submit your complete application documents to the OSAS office during open submission periods. Incomplete submissions will not be processed.

4

Evaluation & Endorsement

OSAS evaluates your application for completeness and compliance, then endorses qualified applicants to the respective scholarship-granting body.

5

Await Results & Comply with Conditions

Results are released based on the granting body's schedule. Once awarded, scholars must comply with all conditions including maintaining academic standing for renewal.

Common Requirements

Documents You'll Need

Prepare these documents in advance. Specific programs may require additional documents.

Certificate of Enrollment

Official COE from the Registrar's Office for the current semester.

Official Transcript / Grades

Certified true copy of grades from the previous semester or school year.

Income Certificate

Barangay-issued certification of family income or ITR for income-based programs.

Good Moral Certificate

Issued by the Guidance Office or the Registrar's Office.

2x2 ID Photos

Recent photos with white background, usually 2–4 copies required.

Accomplished Application Form

Program-specific form available from the OSAS office or official scholarship website.
